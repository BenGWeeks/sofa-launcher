= Cold launch verification — 2026-11-10

The real bundled Sofa main/preload/renderer ran fullscreen in a 1280×720 Xephyr
X11 server with its own kwin_x11, temporary HOME/XDG directories and private
D-Bus session. The final run also updated that bus's activation environment to
the temporary directories and nested display. No KDE settings were intentionally
changed. A red, mapped `_NET_WM_WINDOW_TYPE_DOCK` native X11 window stood in for
Plasma's panel. No streaming services were opened.

== Cases and evidence

The "before" case ran unchanged origin/main launching an Electron desktop fixture
that maps at 600×400, then requests fullscreen after 1.8 seconds; the "after" case
ran the fixed launcher with the same fixture. (Screen recordings were made but are
not kept in the repository.) They illustrate Home versus the new black cover; they
do not demonstrate a reproduced TV flash.

The Edge case ran the fixed launcher cold-starting installed Edge with a fresh
temporary `--user-data-dir`, `--class=sofa-cold-probe`, `--start-fullscreen`,
`--no-first-run`, `--no-default-browser-check`, `--disable-background-networking`,
`--password-store=basic` and a local `--app=data:text/html,...` green test page.
It is configured as a custom desktop app so the production web-URL validator
need not permit data URLs. The baseline also ran this cold Edge case.

The fixture used its own `sofa-cold-delayed` WM_CLASS, distinct from Sofa's class.
Both cases went through Home's tile shortcut, the real AppManager process spawn,
discovery and activation path, and the production launchCover controller.

`*-*.json` contains approximately 50 ms samples of
`xprop -root _NET_ACTIVE_WINDOW _NET_CLIENT_LIST_STACKING`, active-window state
and class, cover geometry from `xdotool getwindowgeometry --shell`, and the
cover's `WM_NORMAL_HINTS` and `_NET_WM_STATE`. Stacks run bottom to top.
`cover_visible` distinguishes the dedicated cover from fallback Home in the
`cover` field. `panel_above` compares their indexes only when both are managed;
`summary.json` also checks that no visible final cover is absent from the stack.

Both fixed cases have 100 samples, maximum interval 51 ms. The delayed case has
40 cover-visible samples, Edge has eight: zero have the panel above the cover,
and every visible cover is managed and exactly 1280×720 at (0,0). The target
became active and fullscreen and the cover disappeared. The recording also
checks pixels, including the last screen row/column. All test launcher, fixture,
Edge, Xephyr, KWin and test-session helper processes were stopped afterwards.

== Limits

The unchanged build did not reproduce the reported TV flash in this nested
session. Its older samples include a screenshot-induced 154 ms gap in the Edge
case. The fixed measurements take screenshots from the video afterwards and
have no such pause. These are sampled observations, not a proof about every
composited frame or every real application.

Physical TV/Plasma behaviour, first-run dialogs, Flatpak apps, multi-monitor and
HiDPI layouts were not exercised. Eight-second expiry, a hung open/X11 probe,
unrelated windows, a normal window becoming fullscreen, and overlapping launches
are covered by deterministic unit tests. In the recorded build, nonfullscreen
apps released through the timeout. The review fix now releases those apps once
a matching mapped window is active; fullscreen launches still wait for the
fullscreen state.
